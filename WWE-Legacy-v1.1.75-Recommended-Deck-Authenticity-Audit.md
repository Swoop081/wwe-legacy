# WWE Legacy v1.1.75 — Recommended Deck Authenticity Audit

This audit is the pre-balance realism gate for all active/internal Superstar recommended decks. It checks 60-page integrity, canonical signature/finisher coverage, deck move identity, and whether generic cards are legitimate shared moves rather than arbitrary filler. Shared real-life moves are intentionally not duplicated solely to make a card Superstar-exclusive.

## Audit decisions

- **AUTHENTICITY CHANGE** — a concrete card/deck/data correction was made in v1.1.75.
- **SHARED-CORE REVIEWED** — the deck uses fewer Superstar-exclusive move IDs, but its key real-life offense is already represented by legitimate shared cards.
- **AUTHORED CORE PASS** — the deck already has a sufficiently authored Superstar-specific move package and all declared signatures resolve in the 60-page recommendation.

## v1.1.75 concrete corrections

- **Jade Cargill:** adds Reverse Alabama Slam and Eye of the Storm as new Survivor Series cards and moves both into her 60-page recommendation alongside Pump Kick, Spinebuster and Jaded.
- **Logan Paul:** adds Prime Splash as a new RAW Series 1 Trademark card and moves it into his recommendation. Flipping Lariat remains available as RAW booster depth instead of forced recommendation filler.
- **Tiffany Stratton:** increases current-real-life weighting by replacing generic Body Slam slots with the already-existing Alabama Slam and Falcon Arrow cards; Swanton Bomb and Prettiest Moonsault Ever remain.
- **Chelsea Green:** corrects the display identity of her Trademark from “I’m Prettier” to WWE’s current “Un-Pretty-Her” naming while preserving the existing canonical card ID/collector slot.
- **Charlotte Flair:** removes a redundant shared Spear declaration from her Superstar signature metadata because her recommendation already carries the dedicated Charlotte Flair Spear card.

## Important shared-card findings

- **Jey Uso:** Superkick, Spear, Running Hip Attack, Suicide Dive and Uso Splash are already in the recommendation. They stay shared where appropriate; no fake duplicate “Jey-only” versions are created.
- **Dominik Mysterio:** 619, Three Amigos and Frog Splash are all already present. 619 and Three Amigos remain legitimate shared identities.
- **Finn Bálor:** Sling Blade, Shotgun Dropkick, 1916 and Coup de Grâce are all already present.
- **LA Knight:** Burning Hammer, top-rope Diving Elbow Drop and BFT are already present.
- **IYO SKY:** Hurricanrana, Meteora, Bullet Train Attack and Over the Moonsault are already represented.
- **Nia Jax:** Samoan Drop and Leg Drop families are already heavily represented alongside Annihilator.
- **Liv Morgan:** Codebreaker and Oblivion are already represented and weighted.
- **Jake Roberts:** Short-Arm Clothesline, running knee/knee-lift offense and DDT family are already represented.

## Gate for the balance pass

All 97 recommendations must remain 60 pages, every declared signature must resolve, and any future move-card additions must be checked against real wrestler usage before numerical balance changes are made.
