# WWE Legacy v1.1.83 — NXT + Ruthless Aggression Move Identity Audit

This audit was triggered by the Kendal Grey card-data inversion discovered during v1.1.82. Every Superstar-specific Move used by the eight NXT — Series 1 Superstars and eight Ruthless Aggression — Series 1 Superstars was rechecked for move identity, Finisher/Trademark role, move type, Method, submission status, posture requirements, deck usage, clone residue and broken search links.

## NXT — Series 1

### Kendal Grey — corrected
- WWE’s 2025–2026 results repeatedly identify **Shades of Grey** as Kendal’s match-finishing move.
- The prior card had the right Finisher slot mechanically but was incorrectly named **Olympic Slam**. It is now **Shades of Grey** while preserving canonical card ID `kendal-grey-olympic-slam` so existing artwork references do not break.
- **Ankle Lock** remains a grounded Technical Submission Trademark rather than the old incorrect Strike/aerial-style implementation.
- Olympic Takedown and Rolling German Suplex remain grappling Trademarks.

### Tony D’Angelo — corrected
- WWE’s current Superstar profile and 2026 NXT results identify **Dead To Rights** as Tony’s signature match-ending move.
- Existing Finisher card `tony-dangelo-forget-about-it` is renamed **Dead to Rights** with its ID preserved.
- **Fisherman Buster** is corrected from an impossible chest-damage submission into a Technical grapple Trademark.
- Crowbar’s old search text is retargeted from the obsolete `Forget About It` name to **Dead to Rights**.

### Jaida Parker — corrected
- WWE 2026 results repeatedly show **Hipnotic → Deja Vu** as Jaida’s winning sequence.
- Existing `jaida-parker-corner-spinebuster` Finisher is renamed **Deja Vu** with the ID preserved.
- **Running Hip Attack** is corrected from Technical grapple to Strike/Strike and grounds the opponent.
- Samoan Drop’s Finisher-search chain now targets **Deja Vu**.
- Removed an inherited search hook for nonexistent `Jaida Parker Driver` rather than inventing a fake card.

### Kelani Jordan — corrected
- WWE 2025–2026 results explicitly identify **One of a Kind Split-Legged Moonsault** as Kelani’s match-ending move.
- Split-Legged Moonsault is now the aerial Finisher.
- **450 Splash** is now an Agility aerial Trademark rather than the Finisher.
- **Springboard Cutter** is an Agility aerial Trademark usable against a standing opponent and grounds the opponent.
- Recommended-deck copies are reweighted so the new Finisher is not overstocked.

### Mason Rook — audited / provisional
- Current WWE results show Blue Thunderbomb, avalanche powerslam and sit-out powerbomb in his offense.
- WWE’s June 30, 2026 result explicitly says a massive sit-out powerbomb was **not** enough to win.
- The current game still uses Sit-Out Powerbomb as a provisional Finisher because WWE.com has not yet established a repeated named match-ending move for Rook. No speculative rename was made.

### Tatum Paxley — verified
- WWE results repeatedly identify **Cemetery Drive** as Tatum’s match-ending move.
- Current Finisher role is retained.
- Psycho Trap, Bridging German Suplex and Diving Knee Drop retain their supporting Trademark roles.

### Lexis King — corrected
- WWE 2025–2026 results repeatedly identify **Coronation DDT** as Lexis King’s match-ending move.
- Existing `lexis-king-coronation-neckbreaker` card is renamed **Coronation DDT** and promoted to the Finisher while preserving its ID.
- King’s Landing is demoted to a Technical grapple Trademark.
- Recommended-deck copies are reweighted around the corrected Finisher hierarchy.

### Zilla Fatu — corrected mechanically / role provisional
- **Island Driver** was incorrectly represented as a diving aerial grounded-only move. It is now a no-Method grapple Finisher that grounds the opponent.
- Removed an inherited search hook for nonexistent `Zilla Fatu’s Running Leg Drop` rather than inventing a fake card.
- WWE’s current NXT results have not yet established a repeated named match-ending move for Zilla, so the Finisher name remains provisional rather than being guessed.

## Ruthless Aggression — Series 1

### John Cena — verified
- Attitude Adjustment and STF remain Finishers; Protobomb and Five Knuckle Shuffle remain Trademarks.
- No identity inversion found.

### Randy Orton — corrected rules consistency
- WWE identifies **RKO** as Randy Orton’s signature move; RKO remains a Finisher.
- Punt Kick remains a secondary Finisher.
- Both Finishers now correctly use no Method, matching WWE Legacy’s global Finisher rule.

### Batista — verified
- WWE identifies **Batista Bomb** as Batista’s signature move; current Finisher role is retained.
- No major move-type inversion found.

### JBL — corrected
- WWE identifies **Clothesline From Hell** as JBL’s signature move and describes it as a lariat/clothesline strike.
- It is corrected from `grapple` to `strike` while remaining his no-Method Finisher.

### Eddie Guerrero — major correction
- WWE lists **Frog Splash, Lasso from El Paso and Three Amigos** as Eddie’s signature moves.
- Frog Splash was incorrectly implemented as a Submission Finisher. It is now an aerial no-Method Finisher against a grounded opponent.
- Lasso from El Paso was incorrectly a normal grapple. It is now a grounded Technical Submission Trademark.
- Removed inherited `Jericho-exclusive` wording.
- Hurricanrana is no longer incorrectly grounded-only.

### Edge — corrected move types
- Spear remains Edge’s Finisher.
- Edge-O-Matic and Impaler DDT are corrected from strike-style data to grapples.
- Edgecution is no longer incorrectly grounded-only.

### Jeff Hardy — corrected
- WWE lists **Swanton Bomb, Whisper in the Wind and Twist of Fate** as Jeff Hardy signature moves.
- Swanton Bomb is now correctly typed as an aerial no-Method Finisher.
- Whisper in the Wind is now Agility/aerial rather than Strike/strike.

### Rob Van Dam — major correction retained/refined
- WWE lists **Five-Star Frog Splash** and **Van Daminator** among RVD’s signature moves and historical match reports repeatedly show Five-Star Frog Splash ending matches.
- Five-Star Frog Splash is the aerial no-Method Finisher.
- Split-Legged Moonsault is an Agility aerial Trademark.
- Rolling Thunder is Agility/aerial.
- Van Daminator is Strike/strike.
- Removed inherited search for nonexistent `Rob Van Dam Driver`.
- The corrected move hierarchy initially pushed RVD to 61.35% in the full matrix; The Whole F’n Show ability was trimmed from 3 uses to 2 without weakening the authentic cards. Final deep result: **58.45%**.

## Cross-set data-integrity checks
- 16/16 Superstar-specific move packages inspected.
- No NXT/Ruthless Finisher retains a Method requirement in runtime data.
- Finisher rarity roles were realigned after swaps.
- No foreign-Superstar clone name remains in the audited rules text.
- Broken search targets to nonexistent Jaida, Zilla and RVD cards were removed.
- Canonical card IDs were preserved for renamed existing cards wherever possible to protect Card Studio and artwork links.
- `rebuild-validate` remains at 98 Superstars / 98 decks / 864 gameplay cards / 0 orphans / 0 issues.
