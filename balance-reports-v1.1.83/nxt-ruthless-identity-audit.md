# WWE Legacy v1.1.83 — NXT + Ruthless Aggression Identity Audit

## Scope

- Audited all **16 Superstars** across NXT — Series 1 and Ruthless Aggression — Series 1.
- Checked every Superstar-specific Move/Trademark/Finisher for move type, Method, submission state, grounded/standing state, search links, Finisher hierarchy and recommended-deck role.
- Canonical card IDs were preserved wherever possible so existing Card Studio/art references remain stable.
- Inherited search effects pointing at cards that do not exist were removed rather than inventing replacement moves.

## High-confidence corrections

### NXT — Series 1

- **Kendal Grey:** Ankle Lock is a Submission Trademark; Olympic Slam is the Grapple Finisher.
- **Tony D’Angelo:** Fisherman Buster corrected from fake Submission to Grapple Trademark; `Forget About It` display identity corrected to **Dead to Rights**; Crowbar search target updated.
- **Jaida Parker:** Corner Spinebuster display identity corrected to **Deja Vu**; Running Hip Attack corrected to Strike; removed inherited search for nonexistent `Jaida Parker Driver`; Samoan Drop search updated to Deja Vu.
- **Kelani Jordan:** Split-Legged Moonsault corrected to Aerial Finisher; 450 Splash becomes Agility/Aerial Trademark; Springboard Cutter no longer incorrectly requires a grounded opponent; deck reweighted to 2 Finisher / 3 450 Splash.
- **Lexis King:** Coronation card corrected to **Coronation DDT** and promoted to Grapple Finisher; King’s Landing becomes Technical Grapple Trademark; deck counts reweighted around corrected roles.
- **Zilla Fatu:** Island Driver corrected from Diving Aerial/grounded-only to Grapple Finisher; removed inherited search for nonexistent `Zilla Fatu’s Running Leg Drop`.
- Mason Rook and Tatum Paxley had no high-confidence role inversion requiring a forced change in this pass.

### Ruthless Aggression — Series 1

- **Randy Orton:** RKO and Punt Kick now obey the global Finisher rule with no Method requirement.
- **JBL:** Clothesline from Hell corrected from Grapple to Strike Finisher.
- **Eddie Guerrero:** Lasso from El Paso corrected to grounded Submission Trademark; Frog Splash corrected from fake Submission to Aerial Finisher; inherited Jericho wording removed; Hurricanrana no longer incorrectly requires a grounded opponent.
- **Edge:** Edge-O-Matic and Impaler DDT corrected to Grapple move types; Edgecution no longer incorrectly requires a grounded opponent.
- **Jeff Hardy:** Whisper in the Wind corrected to Agility/Aerial; Swanton Bomb corrected to Aerial Finisher.
- **Rob Van Dam:** Five-Star Frog Splash remains the authentic Aerial Finisher; Rolling Thunder corrected to Agility/Aerial; Van Daminator corrected to Strike; removed inherited search for nonexistent `Rob Van Dam Driver`; final power budget C11/D17 after certification trim.
- John Cena and Batista had no comparable high-confidence identity inversion requiring a role change.
