## NXT — Series 1

### Kendal Grey
Already corrected in v1.1.82:
- Ankle Lock is a grounded Submission Trademark.
- Olympic Slam is a damage Grapple Finisher.

### Tony D’Angelo
Corrected:
- Fisherman Buster was incorrectly authored as a submission. It is now a damage Grapple Trademark.
- `Forget About It` display identity is updated to **Dead to Rights**, matching Tony’s current WWE match-ending move.
- Crowbar’s search chain now targets Dead to Rights instead of the retired display name.

### Jaida Parker
Corrected:
- Previous Corner Spinebuster Finisher identity is replaced by **Deja Vu**.
- Running Hip Attack was incorrectly classified as a Technical Grapple. It is now a Strike / Strike move.
- Removed a cloned search hook to nonexistent `Jaida Parker Driver`.

### Kelani Jordan
Corrected:
- **One of a Kind Split-Legged Moonsault** is now the Finisher.
- 450 Splash is now an Agility Aerial Trademark.
- Springboard Cutter no longer incorrectly requires an already-grounded opponent.
- Recommended deck changed to 2 Split-Legged Moonsault Finishers and 3 450 Splash Trademarks.

### Mason Rook
Audited, no forced finisher rewrite:
- WWE sources confirm his extreme power offense, moonsault and multiple powerbomb/slam variants.
- Current Sit-Out Powerbomb Finisher remains provisionally authored because WWE evidence has not yet established one definitive recurring match-ending move strongly enough to justify guessing.
- Keep on identity watch as his NXT move set develops.

### Tatum Paxley
Confirmed:
- Cemetery Drive is correctly the Finisher.
- No high-confidence identity inversion found in the rest of her authored package.

### Lexis King
Corrected:
- **Coronation DDT** is now the Finisher.
- King’s Landing moves to Trademark status.
- Recommended deck now carries 2 Coronation DDT Finishers and 5 King’s Landing Trademarks.

### Zilla Fatu
Corrected mechanically:
- Island Driver was incorrectly authored as a grounded diving aerial. It is now a Grapple Finisher that grounds the opponent.
- Removed a cloned search hook to nonexistent `Zilla Fatu’s Running Leg Drop`.
- Current WWE finishing hierarchy remains on watch because his 2026 NXT run is still new.

## Ruthless Aggression — Series 1

### John Cena
Confirmed:
- Attitude Adjustment and STF are correctly represented as Finishers.
- No high-confidence inversion found.

### Randy Orton
Corrected:
- RKO and Punt Kick now obey the global Finisher rule and carry no Method requirement.
- RKO remains the primary signature identity.

### Batista
Confirmed:
- Batista Bomb remains the Finisher.
- Spinebuster / Spear power package remains coherent.
- No high-confidence inversion found.

### JBL
Corrected:
- Clothesline from Hell was incorrectly authored as a Grapple.
- It is now a Strike Finisher.

### Eddie Guerrero
Major correction:
- Frog Splash was incorrectly authored as a Submission Finisher.
- Frog Splash is now an Aerial damage Finisher.
- Lasso from El Paso was incorrectly authored as a standard Grapple.
- Lasso from El Paso is now a grounded Technical Submission Trademark.
- Removed inherited wrong-Superstar rules text.
- Eddie’s Hurricanrana no longer incorrectly requires a grounded opponent.

### Edge
Corrected move categories:
- Edge-O-Matic and Impaler DDT are Grapple-type moves rather than Strikes.
- Edgecution no longer incorrectly requires a grounded opponent.
- Spear remains the Finisher.

### Jeff Hardy
Corrected:
- Whisper in the Wind is now an Agility Aerial Trademark rather than a Strike.
- Swanton Bomb is now an Aerial Finisher rather than a Strike.

### Rob Van Dam
Corrected:
- Five-Star Frog Splash is an Aerial Finisher.
- Rolling Thunder is an Agility Aerial move.
- Van Daminator is a Strike Trademark.
- Split-Legged Moonsault remains an Agility Aerial Trademark.
- Removed a cloned search hook to nonexistent `Rob Van Dam Driver`.
